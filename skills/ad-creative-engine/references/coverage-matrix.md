# The 15-Cell Coverage Matrix

The board. Three production pillars down the side, five buyer-awareness stages across the top. Every live ad occupies exactly one cell. Fifteen cells filled = a complete ladder in a single ad set.

|  | Unaware | Problem-aware | Solution-aware | Product-aware | Most-aware |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **In-house** | | | | | |
| **Creator/UGC** | | | | | |
| **AI** | | | | | |

Fill each cell with the ad name and its 7-day spend share. Empty cells are the work queue.

---

## Reading the two axes

**Pillars answer "who made it and how fast can we get more".** They are a supply-chain decision.

**Stages answer "what job does this ad do".** They are a message decision.

You need both because supply without a ladder gives you fifteen near-identical top-funnel ads, and a ladder without supply gives you a beautiful plan you can't keep stocked.

---

## Stage 1 — Unaware

*They don't yet know they have the problem.* They've normalised it. The ad's only job is to name the thing they've been living with.

**Formats**
- **Stat hook** — a number that reframes something ordinary as a problem. Needs a real, citable source.
- **Founder story** — why you started, what went wrong, what you couldn't find. The most trust per second of any format you own.
- **Hidden-problem education** — "here's what's actually causing that."
- **Category myth-bust** — the thing everyone believes about this category that isn't true.
- **Side-effect / downside reveal** — the popular thing they're already doing has a cost they haven't priced in.

**Pillar fit:** in-house owns founder story. AI is excellent at stat and education statics. Creators do "I had no idea this was even a thing" well.

**What it should achieve:** a hold and a hook-rate that beats account average. Do not judge this cell on last-click ROAS — it is the rung that makes the other rungs possible.

---

## Stage 2 — Problem-aware

*They know the problem. They don't know a solution exists.* The job is to show the other side and start attaching your product to it.

**Formats**
- **Before / after** — the most direct statement of the gap.
- **Feature callout** — one feature, one benefit, plainly.
- **Three reasons why** — three benefits, fast cuts, ends on the category not the SKU.
- **"The missing piece"** — slots your product into a routine they already have rather than asking them to change it.
- **Day-in-the-life contrast** — with vs without.

**Pillar fit:** creators own before/after (real bodies, real homes, real results). AI handles feature callouts and three-reasons statics cheaply. In-house does the routine/context piece.

---

## Stage 3 — Solution-aware

*They now know solutions exist and are about to go shopping.* This is where you get chosen or lost. The job is to be the obvious pick before they open a comparison tab.

**Formats**
- **Us vs them** — your product against the category alternative, on the axis you win. Must be accurate.
- **Ingredient / spec breakdown** — the label, the materials, the components. Consistently one of the highest-performing formats, and one almost nobody makes in-house until a creator stumbles into it.
- **Social proof wall** — review quotes over product.
- **Long-form testimonial** — a creator who has genuinely used it over time.
- **"Why we made it this way"** — a build/formulation decision explained.

**Pillar fit:** creators own the testimonial. In-house owns "why we made it this way". AI can assemble the proof wall and comparison statics.

**This is the highest-leverage stage.** If you only have budget to fill three cells properly, fill this row.

---

## Stage 4 — Product-aware

*They've been to the site. They may have added to cart. They haven't bought.* The job is to remove the last reason not to.

**Formats**
- **Limited-time offer** — real deadline, real reason.
- **Bundle / multi-buy** — the value case.
- **Objection-handling testimonial** — a creator naming the exact doubt and answering it.
- **Risk reversal** — guarantee, returns, trial.
- **FAQ answer** — one specific question that stalls people, answered in ten seconds.

**Pillar fit:** in-house owns offer and guarantee (you control the terms). Creators own objection handling. AI produces the offer statics in every placement in minutes.

---

## Stage 5 — Most-aware

*They're sold on the concept and on you. They just haven't done it.* Short, direct, one instruction.

**Formats**
- **Direct offer** — product, price, buy.
- **Cart reminder** — the item they left.
- **Final call** — the deadline, once.
- **Restock / low stock** — only when true.
- **Shipping cut-off** — dated, and only when it matters (seasonal).

**Pillar fit:** AI and in-house. Creators are wasted here — you're not building trust, you're closing.

**Keep this row small and honest.** Fake urgency here is where consumer-law problems start, and it teaches your best customers to distrust you.

---

## Rules for filling the board

1. **One angle per cell.** If two ads could swap headlines and still make sense, they're the same ad — merge them and free a cell.
2. **Fill the row before you deepen a cell.** Coverage beats depth until you're at 15/15.
3. **Diversity is in the concept, not the edit.** New background, new music, same claim = still one angle.
4. **Assign a pillar to a cell by what the cell needs**, not by what's easiest to make this week. The reason accounts drift to 80% AI is that AI is easiest, and the reason those accounts stop converting is that AI doesn't carry trust.
5. **Once at 15/15, deepen the cells earning spend** — a second and third angle where the money is going, while keeping every other cell alive.

---

## Beyond 15

At higher spend the board grows on a third axis: **avatar**. Each new avatar gets its own board, because a person who can't fall asleep and a person who wakes up groggy are not the same buyer and don't share an Unaware rung. Don't open a second board until the first plateaus — see `creative-briefs.md` § Personification.

At that point the count is `pillars × stages × avatars`. Three avatars is 45 live creatives. That is what a genuinely scaled account looks like, and it is why the creator squad exists.
